RM WORKRM STORY

Born in Romania in 1981, Razvan started drawing before he could walk. Art direction seemed like the obvious destination. He had other plans: becoming a vet and helping animals in Africa, or wherever he was needed. A chemistry exam put an end to that particular adventure. Art school followed, then a degree in Art Pedagogy.

Before advertising, he sold paintings and tried his hand at set design, television art direction, sound, camera work, furniture design and photography. Different tools, same curiosity: how do you turn an idea into something people can see, feel and remember?

With 22 years in advertising, he has worked as an art director, copywriter, creative director, photographer, illustrator and director, collaborating with international networks and independent agencies in Romania and abroad. His experience includes DDB, BBDO, JWT, Jandl, Godmother, Tempo and Flying Elephant. Big teams, small teams, thick brand manuals, blank pages. Plenty of challenges, and plenty of fun.

His client list spans toys, technology, banking, cars, food, drinks, culture and social causes. It includes LEGO, Samsung, Google, Mercedes-Benz, Ford, SEAT, Pepsi, Lipton, Tuborg, Prigat, Carlsberg, Milka, Oreo, Toblerone, 7Days, Raiffeisen Bank, Raiffeisen Leasing, CEC Bank, Alpha Bank, Terapia, Bayer Aspirin, OMV Petrom, E.ON, Carrefour, H&M, Meze Audio, Greenpeace, Wikipedia, Cărturești, Jurnalul Național and many more. Recent portfolio work also includes BRD and Dăruiește Viață.

Along the way, his work has earned recognition at Cannes Lions, D&AD, Clio, Golden Drum, Epica, PIAF and the Effie Awards in Romania and Slovakia. The awards are welcome. Finding the right idea is still the part he enjoys most.

Outside advertising, Razvan is the owner and curator of the Hellboy Museum, a collection of rare pieces, comics and original movie props dedicated to Mike Mignola’s character. He also collects old comics and toys, watches a lot of films and owns more superhero and horror T-shirts than he probably needs. Tattoos, earrings, a braided beard and hair growing since 1997 complete the picture.

Always curious. Usually drawing. Still making things.
