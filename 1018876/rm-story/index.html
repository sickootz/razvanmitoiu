RM WORKRM STORY

Creative director, art director, illustrator and maker. With 23 years in advertising, Razvan brings a hands-on approach to ideas, images and the stories that connect them.

Before the brief

Born in Romania in 1981, he started drawing before he could walk. Becoming a vet was the original plan: helping animals in Africa, or wherever he was needed. A chemistry exam changed the itinerary. Art school followed, then a degree in Art Pedagogy from the George Enescu University of Arts in Iași.

Before advertising, he sold paintings and worked in set design, television art direction, sound, camera work, furniture design and photography. That mix of disciplines still shapes the way he works: curious about the idea, attentive to the craft and happy to get his hands dirty.

23 years of ideas

His advertising career began at Ars Advertising, working with industrial and corporate clients whose brand manuals could run to more than 150 pages. He went on to work with Speed Promotion, GAV Scholz & Friends, DDB Romania, Griffon & Swans and Graffiti BBDO before moving to Bratislava to join Jandl.

Back in Bucharest, his experience grew through creative leadership roles at Godmother, Tempo Advertising, Flying Elephant and Dentsu Creative Romania, alongside freelance collaborations. Across those teams, he has worked as an art director, copywriter, creative director, photographer, illustrator and director.

Today, he works independently across advertising, brand consulting, visual identity, print, packaging, illustration and copywriting. Recent collaborations include Samsung, Dăruiește Viață, BRD, OMV Petrom, Auchan, Gerovital, Aquavia and Mixtopia.

Beyond advertising

Razvan is also the owner and curator of the Hellboy Museum, with over 1,000 pieces, including rare collectibles, comics and original movie props dedicated to Mike Mignola’s character.

When he is not working on a brief, he is likely to be hunting for old comics and horror toys, sculpting, painting or watching a film. Tattoos, earrings, a braided beard and hair growing since 1997 complete the picture.

Always curious. Usually drawing. Still making things.
